WHAT HAPPENED

Four pillars of the edition

Every space of MeetUp Pro 3.0 was built to turn encounters into opportunities.

Business Fair

Exhibition booths providing direct access to showcase products and connect with potential partners and clients.

B2B Meetings

Strategic networking sessions connecting businesses with investors, partners, and industry leaders.

Discussion Panels

Expert-led panels covering investment readiness, entrepreneurship ecosystems, and the future of work.

Job Fair

Career opportunities with hands-on workshops including THE V MAKER for youth and specialized sessions.

ON THE 3.0 STAGE

The discussion panels

Four panels bringing together founders, investors, corporates and creators around the region’s real challenges.

PANEL 01

Unlocking Capital: The Path to Investment Readiness

Discover what investors look for and how to prepare your startup for funding. Experts share key insights on building credibility, crafting strong pitches, and navigating Tunisia’s investment landscape.

  • MODERATOR Sawsen Haj Amor
    AI Journalist · Host of “Business Talk” · CEO of YOU.Branded
  • PANELIST Rym Gmati
    Avocate d’affaires — Private Equity, Venture Capital, Gouvernance & lutte contre la corruption
  • PANELIST Iyadh Hammami
    CPA & Finance Manager @ Deloitte
PANEL 02

Entrepreneurial Ecosystems: Expectations vs. Realities

An honest discussion about the current struggles of Tunisian entrepreneurs. Speakers uncover the gap between dreams and reality and how resilience and innovation can drive a new wave of success.

  • MODERATOR Sawsen Haj Amor
    AI Journalist · Host of “Business Talk” · CEO of YOU.Branded
  • PANELIST Adel Chouari
    Directeur Général du Centre National du Registre des Entreprises
  • PANELIST Maha Mgaieth
    Sales Coach and Mentor · Project Manager
  • PANELIST Khouloud Abid
    CEO & Founder AUZY · Innovator in Mental Health Tech
PANEL 03

From Vision to Venture: Turning Ideas into Startups

Learn how to turn ideas into successful startups. Founders and innovators share their journeys and strategies to validate concepts, build teams, and bring bold visions to life.

  • MODERATOR Jasser Riahi
    Founder & Chief Executive Officer, Maana
  • PANELIST Bilel Ouirsighni
    Crowdfunding & Monetization · Helping MENA Creators Monetize & Scale
PANEL 04

Workplace 2030: The Gen Z Effect & The Skills That Matter

Explore how Gen Z is reshaping the world of work. This panel dives into how their values of purpose, flexibility, and authenticity are redefining careers and collaboration across industries.

  • MODERATOR Mustapha Boufares
    Fintech Lecturer · Founder @ DFI Blockchain · Speaker
  • PANELIST Ala The Vmaker
    Tech and visuals influencer
  • PANELIST Rayen Bouajaja
    CEO at Lyceena · Transforming Education in Tunisia
  • PANELIST Amal Lahoual
    International Recruitment Coordinator · Talent Acquisition · Global Hiring Strategies
